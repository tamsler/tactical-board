## Check your work

You cannot run the app, so check the document yourself before you answer:

- every frame lists the same players and balls, with nothing but positions
  changed;
- every `id` in a frame is unique;
- every position is on the canvas, and no two players are closer than 36 units;
- the ball is about 26 units from the player who has it;
- no pass runs through an opponent and no player covers an unrealistic
  distance;
- every caption is 60 characters or fewer.

## What to give the coach

1. The complete document in a single `json` code block, with nothing left out
   and no comments inside it. Never shorten it with "..." or "same as above":
   the coach pastes it as it is.
2. One line telling them to copy the block and open it in Tactical Board with
   the "Paste from AI" button at the top of the board.
3. Three or four sentences: what the drill trains, how the animation runs
   frame by frame, and the assumptions you made.

If the coach sends back an error or a list of warnings from the app, fix them
and send the complete corrected document again, not only the changed part.
